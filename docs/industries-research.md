# Industries page: content basis

The Industries page was checked against the supplied 2026 Skyriders brochure, the site's existing service descriptions, and these primary sources on 11 October 2026:

- [Skyriders company and project overview](https://ropeaccess.co.za/) — power generation, mining, construction, petrochemical and industrial experience; examples of stack coatings, NDT, bolting, roof maintenance and deluge-system work.
- [Skyriders concrete services](https://ropeaccess.co.za/services/concrete-services/) — inspection, testing, repair and protection of cooling towers, silos and chimney stacks.
- [IRATA risk assessment guidance](https://irata.org/uploads/documents/International_Code_of_Practice_%28ICOP%29_-_English_TC-102ENG.pdf) and [rescue planning guidance](https://irata.org/news/article/new-icop-annex-R) — the scope and worksite hazards should shape access and rescue planning.
- [IRATA renewable energy overview](https://irata.org/page/renewable-energy) and [GWO advanced rescue standard](https://www.globalwindsafety.org/standards/advanced-rescue-training-standard) — wind assets can involve specialist access and distinct competence and rescue requirements.

The copy separates established Skyriders power-generation work from renewable-site enquiries. It does not claim blade repair, turbine certification or solar installation services. The mining and petrochemical section names their different site controls while keeping them under the one category used on the homepage. Service links point to existing, documented offerings.
